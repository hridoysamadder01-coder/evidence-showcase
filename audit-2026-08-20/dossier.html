What is not established

The boundaries are stated plainly — not as deficiencies, but because they are what make the verified claims trustworthy. Absence of evidence here is absence of a precondition (users, time, a team, institutions), not evidence of incapacity.

  • Production scale / real-user load — deployment is proven; internet-scale traffic and multi-pharmacy concurrency are not.
  • Multi-year longevity — the visible window is ~10.5 weeks; it supports iteration-density claims, not durability claims.
  • Independent human code review & team leadership — structurally absent (solo + AI); an unknown, not a failing.
  • Independent security certification — hardening work is evidenced; third-party certification is not.
  • "World-class" — this is evaluator judgment, not an established fact; there is no population benchmark that converts it into a measurement. The work is presented so the reader can inspect it and reach their own conclusion.
  • Line-level authorship — not provable in either direction; what is evidenced is ownership of the loop.
